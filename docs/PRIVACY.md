# Privacy and data handling

The demonstrator stores account identity, organization membership, vehicle
identifiers, device metadata, decision evidence, coarse operational events,
notifications, GPS points, and audit records. It does not store face images,
raw breath samples, or biometric templates.

Production deployments must publish a jurisdiction-specific notice, define
retention periods, restrict precise location to authorized roles, encrypt data
in transit and at rest, support export/deletion requests where legally
required, document emergency disclosure, and obtain explicit consent for any
biometric or continuous-location feature.
